These are the documents you should ingest into the Qdrant vector
database (collection: "munger_knowledge") using workflow 02.

The prepare_documents.py script will download the freely available
sources automatically. Documents behind paywalls require manual
acquisition.

TIER 1: HIGHEST PRIORITY — Core Munger Philosophy (Free/Easy)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[SPEECH] "The Psychology of Human Misjudgment" (1995)
  - Delivered at Harvard University
  - The definitive Munger text on cognitive biases and mental models
  - Full text freely available at many educational sites
  - Search: "Munger psychology human misjudgment full text"
  - Also reproduced in full in Poor Charlie's Almanack

[SPEECH] "A Lesson on Elementary Worldly Wisdom" (1994)
  - USC Marshall School of Business
  - Introduces the "latticework of mental models" concept
  - Full text: search "Munger elementary worldly wisdom USC 1994 transcript"

[SPEECH] "Practical Thought About Practical Thought?" (1996)
  - USC Law School Commencement Address
  - Demonstrates his thinking approach through a Coca-Cola thought experiment
  - Full text widely available online

[SPEECH] "The Need for More Multidisciplinary Thinking" (1998)
  - Harvard Law School
  - On the failures of specialization in education and practice
  - Search "Munger Harvard law 1998 multidisciplinary"

[SPEECH] "Academic Economics: Strengths and Faults After Considering
           Interdisciplinary Needs" (2003)
  - Santa Barbara City College Economics Department lecture
  - Munger's critique of academic economics
  - Full text widely reproduced

[SPEECH] "Breakfast Meeting of the Philanthropy Round Table" (various)
  - On giving, civic virtue, and the duties of the fortunate

TIER 2: HIGH PRIORITY — Annual Letters & Meetings
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[LETTERS] Wesco Financial Annual Reports — Munger's Chairman Letters (1977-2010)
  - Available at: https://www.berkshirehathaway.com/wesco/annual.html
  - These are Munger's own writing, equivalent to Buffett's BRK letters
  - Cover investing philosophy, business analysis, financial markets
  - STRONGLY RECOMMENDED — this is Munger at his most explicit

[TRANSCRIPTS] Daily Journal Corporation Annual Meetings (2014-2023)
  - Q&A sessions with Munger acting as chairman
  - Available via: http://www.gurufocus.com (search "DJCO annual meeting")
  - Also on ValueInvestorClub, SeekingAlpha, and YouTube transcripts
  - Topics: China, AI, banking, investing, mental models, life philosophy
  - The 2021 meeting transcript is particularly rich

[TRANSCRIPTS] Berkshire Hathaway Annual Meetings — Munger Portions (1994-2023)
  - Full transcripts at: https://www.morningstar.com (search Berkshire annual meeting)
  - Focus on Munger's specific responses (he spoke ~20-30% of the time)
  - Key years: 1999 (internet bubble), 2008 (crisis), 2015-2020 (recent wisdom)

[BOOK] Poor Charlie's Almanack (Peter Kaufman, ed., 2005 / expanded 2023)
  - The definitive Munger anthology
  - Contains: biographical essays, 11 talks, Munger's reading list, artwork
  - NOT free but widely available. The 2023 expanded edition added DJCO material.
  - This is the single most important document to ingest. Buy it.
  - Key sections: "The Munger Approach to Life, Learning, and Decision Making"
    and all 11 talks (many overlap with Tier 1 speeches above)

[BOOK] "Charlie Munger: The Complete Investor" (Tren Griffin, 2015)
  - A synthesis of Munger's principles by a longtime Munger student
  - Not Munger's own words, but a reliable secondary source
  - Good for structured topic coverage

[INTERVIEW] Charlie Munger: A Conversation with the Vice Chairman of
             Berkshire Hathaway (various dates, Stripe Press 2023)
  - Available at: https://press.stripe.com/poor-charlies-almanack
  - Selected as part of the free digital version

[INTERVIEW] Acquired Podcast — Charlie Munger (November 2023)
  - His last major interview, conducted months before his death
  - Transcript available on Acquired.fm
  - Covers: China, AI, banking, Berkshire succession, his life
  - URL: https://www.acquired.fm/episodes/charlie-munger

DOCUMENT PREPARATION NOTES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Format: Plain text (.txt) works best for ingestion. If you have PDFs,
use pdfplumber or pdf2text to extract (see prepare_documents.py).

File naming convention:
  munger_[year]_[short_title].txt
  e.g. munger_1995_psychology_misjudgment.txt
       munger_2003_academic_economics.txt
       munger_wesco_letters_1977_2010.txt

Chunk sizes in the ingestion workflow:
  - 1200 characters per chunk, 200 overlap
  - Each speech is typically 15,000-80,000 characters
  - Wesco letters combined: ~300,000 characters
  - Total estimated corpus: 800,000 - 1,500,000 characters
  - At 1200 chars/chunk: approximately 700-1250 chunks
  - Qdrant free tier handles this easily

Metadata tags used in ingestion (for potential filtered retrieval):
  - category: mental_models | investing_philosophy | investing_current |
               wisdom_life | economics | biography | misc
  - source: speech | annual_report | annual_meeting | book | interview | manual
  - year: [year or year range as string]
  - title: [document title]
